About SOAR Bio

U.S. life science is bloated. Life science innovation in the U.S. is slow and expensive. We've lost our way.

In contrast, life science innovation in China is fast, inexpensive, and here now.

To stay competitive, we need to go back to our entrepreneurial roots and train the next generation of life science leaders to do more with less. But scrappy life science founder/CEOs can't be trained in classrooms, just like a new doctor can't learn to do heart surgery from books alone. What's required is hands-on skill transfer through daily mentorship: a life science apprenticeship.

And there's no better place to do that than the Greater Washington area, home to storied institutions that produce abundant technology and talent. Greater Washington has the ingredients to become the national model for a scrappy life science startup ecosystem.

At SOAR Bio, we build great companies from scratch by pairing seasoned entrepreneurs with technically-gifted entrepreneurial fellows. Our mission is to translate the region's most promising discoveries into commercial life science products. We do that by cultivating the region's most promising talent into life science leaders who can do more with less.

Our Process

🌱

Seed

Generate new life science concepts based on unmet needs and local science.

🔬

Optimize

Refine promising candidates with translational expertise and rapid iteration.

📈

Advance

Launch the best assets as new life science companies led by skilled entrepreneurs.

🔄

Repeat

Iterate continuously to build a portfolio of transformative life science companies.

SOAR Bio Seeds, Optimizes, and Advances life science programs internally, then Repeats the process. We pride ourselves on operational efficiency. We have in-house expertise in IP, preclinical, regulatory, manufacturing, quality, translational, clinical operations, strategy and marketing. Our IP is generated internally but also can be in-licensed, with preference often given to local institutions within the Greater Washington area. Early-career, technically-gifted physicians & scientists with an interest in startups are appointed to leadership roles in our portfolio companies and mentored as entrepreneurial fellows, where they learn the many roles needed to become our next generation of successful scrappy life science CEOs.

Our Founders

Our founders have a proven track record of creating and exiting high-value ventures through acquisitions and public listings. We don't subscribe blindly to the standard U.S. life science mantra "raise money when you can get it, not when you need it". Having sufficient capital is important, but in U.S. life science circles, we tend to spend a lot of time talking about raising capital but too little time talking about how best to spend it. Our Life Science Golden Rule is "If you take care of the pennies, the dollars take care of themselves". We think the best companies are built when raising capital is not an end to itself, but balanced with how best to spend it.
